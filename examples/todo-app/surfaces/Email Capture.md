A personal address the person can forward or send mail to, which turns each message into a [[Task]] in their inbox [[List]]. It exists for to-dos that arrive as email, so they can be captured without leaving the mail client.

## Not responsible for

- **Organising, planning, or completing tasks** — owned by [[Todo App]] instead; Email Capture only creates them.
- **Reminders** — set and delivered in [[Todo App]].

## Provides

- [[Get a task out of my head]] — for [[The Capturer]]
