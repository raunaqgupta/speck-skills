The app the person opens to capture, organise, plan, and complete their [[Task]]s, on whichever device they have to hand. It is where [[List]]s are managed, where today's plan lives, and where [[Reminder]]s are set and arrive.

## Not responsible for

- **Turning forwarded email into tasks** — owned by [[Email Capture]] instead.

## Provides

- [[Get a task out of my head]] — for [[The Capturer]]
- [[Decide what to do today]] — for [[The Planner]]
- [[Make sure a deadline doesn't slip]] — for [[The Planner]]
