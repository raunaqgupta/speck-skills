The Capturer records a to-do the moment it occurs to them so they can stop holding it in memory. Their act ends when the [[Task]] exists; deciding when or whether to do it is not part of this role.

## Distinct from

- **The Planner** — decides which captured tasks get done and when; the Capturer only gets them recorded.
- **Whoever asked for the task** — a colleague or family member may be the source of the to-do, but they don't record it and never touch the product.

## Context of execution

When a to-do surfaces at a moment that can't be spent on it, and the cost of capturing must be close to zero or it won't happen.
