The Planner turns the backlog of open [[Task]]s into a commitment for a specific day and makes sure time-bound tasks surface when they need to. They act on what already exists rather than adding to it, choosing, rescheduling, completing, and setting [[Reminder]]s.

## Distinct from

- **The Capturer** — adds tasks without judging them; the Planner works from what the Capturer left behind.

## Context of execution

When there are more open tasks than time available, or when a task's timing matters more than its place in a list.
