Someone has a time-bound [[Task]] and wants the product, not their memory, to carry it until it matters. The switch is from calendar workarounds or hoping to notice a due date to a [[Reminder]] set on the Task itself.

## Tasks

1. Open the [[Task]] and set a due date if it has one — updates [[Task]]
2. Choose when to be reminded — creates [[Reminder]]
3. Get prompted at that time and act on the Task

See [[Set a reminder]] for the detailed step-by-step.
