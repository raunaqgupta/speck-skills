Someone is interrupted by a stray to-do and wants it off their mind without breaking what they're doing. The switch is from ad-hoc notes they rarely look at again to a single [[List]] inbox they trust, where a [[Task]] can be dropped with only a title.

## Tasks

1. Open the capture affordance from wherever they are
2. Type a title; optionally pick a [[List]] — creates [[Task]]
3. Return to what they were doing; the Task waits in the inbox

See [[Capture a task]] for the detailed step-by-step.

See [[Forward an email as a task]] for the detailed step-by-step.

## Success criteria

- Capture takes under ten seconds from intent to saved.
- Captured tasks are later planned or completed rather than abandoned in the inbox.
